# Highlights

The short version of every release — what you will actually notice.
The full details are in the changelog.

## v1.1.3 – 2026-11-26

- **New look for the VR panel:** dark with blue lines like the VRChat world menus, tabs at the bottom (🌐 Translation · 🖼 Gallery · ⚙ Settings).
- **Side panel on the right:** translation and gallery settings sit in a side panel you can fold away with **›** – then the photo / thumbnails get bigger.
- **Quick switches while translating:** frame gesture (On / 🔫 Auto / Off) and 🥽 Overlay right in the side panel. **🖼 ▲** hides the photo so the translation gets the whole panel.
- **The panel keeps its size:** a small photo, little text or the photo switched off no longer shrink it – the boxes just stay empty.
- **Gallery:** filter, **3 / 4 / 5 columns** and paging in the side panel.
- **Settings like a world menu:** categories on the left, options on the right – 📸 Shot / 🥽 VR fold away.
- **🔫 Auto is the new default** for the frame gesture (new users) – no more frame popping up while holding a two-handed weapon.
- Panels are a bit wider now – if you resized yours before, make it a little bigger once in VR.
- **TranslateGemma for local translation:** Google's translation-only models (4B fast, 12B better, 55 languages) are now in the local image LLM model list, marked with ⭐ – pick one and press *📥 Load model*. They only translate (no explaining or quiz answers).
